# Icona Wormfront

Creata con lo strumento integrato imagegen per il progetto SboMusic.
Uso: icona del progetto Godot, eseguibile Windows e collegamento Start.
`icon.png` e il master trasparente; `icon.ico` deriva dal master tramite
`tools/make_icon.ps1`, con dimensioni 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 e 256 pixel.

Prompt utilizzato:

> Create an original polished PC game app icon for an indie cartoon artillery game called Wormfront. No text, no letters. Two original expressive pink earthworm soldiers back to back, one with turquoise headband and tiny futuristic bazooka, one with orange bandana and round cartoon grenade. Chunky hand painted 2D cartoon shapes, thick dark navy outline, warm highlights, dark teal circular shield background with a small golden explosion silhouette. Square composition, centered compact silhouette, exceptionally readable at 32 pixels, rich professional game launcher icon, not a copy of any existing Worms characters or logo. Transparent outer background beyond the shield.
