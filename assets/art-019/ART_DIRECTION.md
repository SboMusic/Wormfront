## Prompt 1
Use case: stylized-concept. Wide 16:9 hand-painted background ONLY for original cartoon 2D artillery PC game Crateri. Moonlit alpine valley with distant blue ice peaks, layered mist, fir trees and faint aurora, luminous turquoise sky and lavender clouds. Same polished painted cartoon adventure look as a high quality 2D game, restrained contrast so foreground gameplay remains readable. Distant landscape mostly in lower third and edges, roomy sky, no characters, no weapons, no platforms, no UI, no text. Full bleed, no borders, no existing-game logos or characters.

## Prompt 3
Use case: stylized-concept. Production terrain texture atlas for original hand painted 2D cartoon artillery game Crateri. Wide 3:1 canvas divided into THREE equal square panels, no margins no frames no labels. Each square is a full bleed repeating material cross section, flat orthographic, no sky or landscape, same polished cartoon paint style and dark clear organic contours. LEFT square: ancient ruined castle wall, irregular large warm sandstone blocks, cracked masonry, pockets of moss, earth and rubble, gold highlights and dark brown joints. CENTER square: layered blue slate and translucent icy quartz embedded in compact pale earth, cool blue shadows, snowy light seams. RIGHT square: volcanic obsidian rock with warm basalt faceted boulders and narrow glowing orange fissures, charcoal and burgundy earth. These are texture materials that will be clipped to destructible terrain in game, not a scene illustration. Richly painted tactile detail readable at 512 game pixels. No objects, letters, figures or logos.
